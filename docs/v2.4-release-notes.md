# Naviamp 2.4 Release Notes

Prepared for v2.4.0; not yet published.

Naviamp 2.4 improves browsing large music libraries, makes playlist membership easier to manage,
and introduces a shared wide player workspace.

## Library and discovery

Switch between Artists, Albums, and Songs with independent searches and alphabetical navigation.
Albums use a persistent, source-scoped catalog: the first visit indexes the library with progress
feedback, while later visits and restarts reuse the saved snapshot. Refresh keeps it current.
Artist pages separate primary releases from Appears On results and retain expanded sections during
navigation and metadata updates.

Favorite Artists is available on Home and in Home Screen settings. Sort its detail page by name,
favorite activity, or last artist-radio playback. Home section visibility and ordering remain
customizable, with direct dragging and accessible move actions in Settings.

## Playlists

Manage a track's membership across playlists in one editor. Create a playlist without leaving the
editor and apply membership changes without replacing unrelated entries. Smart and non-editable
playlists expose their restrictions. Updated lists, counts, and open details reconcile immediately,
including when older requests finish after an edit.

## Player and appearance

Wide windows can keep the player docked beside Home, Library, Search, Queue, and other browsing
screens. Switch to Full player for larger artwork and track details or lyrics. The saved preference
survives restart and settings sync; narrow windows retain their stacked presentation.

Aurora offers two to five cover colors, a 0–180 degree angle, and Light, Balanced, and Dark tones.
The prior serialized Dark tone retains its original appearance under the Balanced label. Older
settings exports default to three colors, 45 degrees, and Split player layout. Waveform progress
animates between playback updates while respecting pause and seek.

## Compatibility fixes

Windows equalization now uses the bundled BASS FX peaking equalizer, restoring low-frequency
bands rejected by the Windows DX8 API. All hosts consume the same shared frequencies, gain limits,
and bandwidth plan. Bands at or above a stream's Nyquist frequency are omitted rather than retuned.

OpenSubsonic fixes cover playlist replacement and clearing, original downloads and API-error
detection, permissions, lyrics fallback, and listen reporting. Native Navidrome enhancements remain
capability-dependent; this release does not claim live validation against every compatible server.

## Upgrading

- v2.3.1 installations are intended to upgrade in place without an application-data reset.
- Shared migration 24 upgrades database schema 24 to 25, adding favorite-artist activity, artist
  credits, and album catalog snapshots. Initial indexing stores metadata, not audio downloads.
- Portable settings remain part of shared export/import and sync. Out-of-range Aurora values are
  normalized on import.
- Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux remain release targets. The iOS arm64 IPA remains an unsigned
  preview requiring the user's own signing. Desktop packages remain unsigned.

Release verification is tracked in [the stabilization checklist](v2.4-stabilization.md).
