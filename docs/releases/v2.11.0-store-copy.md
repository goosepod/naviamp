# Naviamp v2.11.0 store copy

Draft copy for **2.11.0 (61)**, compared with public v2.10.0. Preparation does not
establish console upload, review, rollout or availability. Keep existing review,
privacy and support fields; use the [distribution handoff](v2.11.0-readiness.md).

## What's new — Apple and Microsoft

Explore more music with Aurral: browse full recommendations and catalog search,
related artists, releases and editions, previews and Trending charts. Follow requests
from waiting for release through acquisition to availability in your Library, even
after reconnecting. Available actions depend on your server and account permissions.

Choose new artwork layouts and clearer repeat controls. This update also improves
internet-radio titles and artwork, playback transitions and paused positions, lyrics,
album playback settings, menus and translations.

Your database upgrades automatically to retain Aurral request history. Update other
devices to 2.11.0 before relying on synchronization of the new artwork settings.
Acquisition cancel/retry remains available through Aurral itself. Full changes and
known issues: https://github.com/goosepod/naviamp/releases/tag/v2.11.0

## Google Play — under 500 characters

Explore Aurral recommendations, catalog search, related artists, releases, previews and Trending charts. Follow request progress after reconnecting. Choose new artwork layouts and keep Android TV controls visible. Enjoy improved radio metadata, playback transitions, paused positions, lyrics and translations. Fix startup when Cast is unavailable. Update other devices to 2.11.0 before syncing new settings. Original Gapless/TV Autoplay reports remain under investigation.

- Check artwork settings, import/export, saved connections, downloads and relaunch.
- Include app/build and reproducible steps with feedback. Simulator checks do not
  establish physical-device background, lock-screen or audio-route acceptance.
