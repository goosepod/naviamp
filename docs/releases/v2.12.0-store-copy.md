# Naviamp v2.12.0 store copy

Store and beta-channel text for the published
[Naviamp v2.12.0](https://github.com/goosepod/naviamp/releases/tag/v2.12.0) release, build 65.
Each section describes changes compared with the previous public release.

## Google Play — under 500 characters

Add an Aurral account as a companion to your music server for recommendations, catalog search, samples and requests without switching servers. Android Auto now leads with Playlists, Recently Played and Radio, and adds Start Radio for the current song. Android TV and Google TV are no longer beta. Mixes for you start a radio station, and Naviamp Connect queues albums correctly and keeps elapsed time moving. Aurral connections now require Aurral 3.x.

## What's new — Microsoft

Naviamp 2.12.0 lets you add an Aurral account as a companion to your music server.
Aurral recommendations, catalog search, samples, monitoring and requests open alongside
your library without switching servers or interrupting playback. You can hide or reorder
the Aurral section on Home like other sections.

Mixes for you now start a radio station seeded from the mix. While you control another
device with Naviamp Connect, choosing a track queues its album or playlist from that track,
and elapsed time keeps advancing. Importing settings keeps this device's volume, output
device and logging settings, and Reconnect restores the imported server.

Aurral connections now require Aurral 3.x. The local database upgrades automatically to
store Aurral companion accounts; existing connections and settings are retained. Full
changes and known issues: https://github.com/goosepod/naviamp/releases/tag/v2.12.0
